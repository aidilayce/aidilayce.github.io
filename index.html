Aytekin

PhD student at Max Planck Institute for Informatics

aaytekin [at] mpi-inf [dot] mpg [dot] de

I am a PhD student in the Visual Computing and Artificial Intelligence Department at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics and Saarland University, advised by Christian Theobalt.

My research focuses on contact-aware 3D perception for dexterous manipulation. I build physically grounded reconstruction methods and visual-tactile representations that recover pose, geometry, and contact from human hand-object interactions, with the goal of turning human interaction data into useful signals for robot learning.
